# Python Chapter 12: Dictionaries, Looking Things Up by Name

Customers ask for prices by name, but a list answers by position. Follow Asha as she learns dictionaries: keys and values, adding, changing and removing items, in and get() with a default, looping with items(), counting by name and nested data. Then watch her build a stock tracker with an AI assistant at all six stages. She challenges its two-list design and catches names printed instead of numbers, a crash it did not predict, and a change that broke her rule.
