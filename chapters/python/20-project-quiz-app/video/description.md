# Python Chapter 20: Boss Level 4, The Tested Quiz App

New staff at the Corner Cafe keep getting prices and allergens wrong. Follow Asha as she builds a staff quiz from three modules and writes its eight tests before the code. An AI assistant helps at all six stages. Her tests catch its confident mistakes: a rule that cannot be tested, a percentage that gives 0, and a shorter grade that changes one sign.
