## What was built
- **Story:** at closing time, nobody at the Corner Cafe knows where the money went. The mission is an expense tracker: Asha types a category and an amount for each expense, then `done`.
- **Project:** a 25-line tracker that:
  - stores each expense as a tuple in a list, and keeps the allowed categories in a set;
  - checks each amount with `replace(".", "", 1).isdigit()` before `float()`;
  - builds totals with `get(key, 0)` and prints a sorted table;
  - finds the biggest category in the same loop, and prints `Biggest: none` for an empty day.
- **Verification of the project:** the tracker is verified with a normal day. Five test copies with typed answers cover an empty day, bad amounts, unknown categories, one expense and two cups.

  - **Test:** the AI lists three test days but forgets the empty day. Day 3 passes only by luck.
